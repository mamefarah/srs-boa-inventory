# M0 Interview Checklist — Bureau Store Procedure Validation

Use with the Property Administration Head, storekeeper/stores officer, Procurement, Finance, Internal Audit and representative requesting directorates.

Do not ask respondents to design the software. Ask them to show how the official/current process actually works and the documents that prove it.

## A. Authority and source documents

1. What is the current Somali Region Stock Management Manual?
2. What is the current Property Administration Manual/directive?
3. Which proclamation/directive/manual currently governs Bureau stores?
4. Are older 2011 regional manuals still valid, amended or replaced?
5. Can copies be provided?
6. Which BoFED office gives authoritative interpretation?

## B. Goods receipt

1. Show the actual current receipt form.
2. What is its official name/number?
3. Who counts delivered goods?
4. Who inspects quality/specification?
5. Is an inspection/acceptance committee required? For which purchases?
6. Can goods enter store before inspection?
7. How are partial acceptance and rejection recorded?
8. Who receives copies of the receipt document?
9. Can Finance pay without the store receipt/acceptance evidence?
10. The Bureau FM Manual and Bureau internal-audit evidence explicitly reference Goods Received Note / Model 19, while the Bureau Procurement Manual separately references inspection/acceptance followed by Stores Receipt Voucher. In current Bureau store operations, are GRN and SRV the same document, separate sequential documents, alternative terminology, or documents used for different purposes? Please show the current forms.

## C. Requisition and issue

1. Show the actual store requisition form.
2. Who may request stock?
3. Who authorizes the request?
4. Show the issue voucher.
5. Which signatures are mandatory?
6. Is partial issue allowed?
7. How is an unfulfilled approved quantity handled?
8. Are project/funding restrictions checked before issue?
9. Is recipient acknowledgement required?

## D. Warehouse transfer

1. Is transfer between Bureau warehouses currently practiced?
2. Show the transfer/dispatch/receipt document.
3. Who authorizes the transfer?
4. Who dispatches?
5. Who acknowledges destination receipt?
6. How is quantity damaged/missing in transit documented?
7. Who resolves discrepancies?

## D-bis. Return to store and supplier return of rejected goods

1. Is returning previously issued stock to a warehouse currently practiced? Show the form.
2. Who authorizes accepting a returned item back into store?
3. What condition/inspection is applied to a returned item before it re-enters usable stock?
4. Show the current form used to return rejected/failed-inspection goods to the supplier.
5. Who authorizes and signs a supplier return?
6. What happens if the supplier does not accept the return?

## E. Stock records

1. Show stock card/bin card/stock register currently used.
2. Which record is maintained by storekeeper vs property officer?
3. Is one card maintained per item, batch, funding source or location?
4. How are corrections made on paper?
5. How are document references linked to each stock-card entry?

## F. Physical count

1. How often are full counts performed?
2. Are cycle/spot counts used?
3. Who appoints the count team?
4. Is the storekeeper part of the count team?
5. Does the count team see book quantity before counting?
6. What documents are produced?
7. What variance requires recount?
8. Who approves shortages/surpluses?

## G. Damage, expiry, obsolete stock and disposal

1. How are damaged/expired/obsolete items identified?
2. Is stock kept physically separate?
3. Show condition/disposal forms.
4. What committee/authority approves disposal?
5. What valuation or thresholds alter approval authority?
6. What evidence is retained after destruction/sale/transfer?

## H. Fixed assets and custody

1. Which items are treated as stock versus fixed assets?
2. At what point does a durable item leave store control and enter fixed-asset register?
3. Show property number/asset registration and custody handover forms.
4. Who is accountable after issue?
5. How are asset transfers/returns/disposals recorded?
6. The Bureau's own FM Manual defines the fixed-asset register threshold as useful life longer than 1 year **and** value of Birr 2,000 or above — this is currently BoA-IMS's working baseline. Does a current Somali Region/BoFED manual or directive state a different figure that should supersede it, and is the test "and" (both conditions) or "or" (either condition)? If a different figure applies, please show the current controlling manual/directive page.

## I. UOM and item coding

1. Is there a standardized item code catalogue?
2. Who creates a new item code?
3. What is the base unit for common items?
4. Can one item be received by bag/carton and issued by kg/piece?
5. If yes, who defines conversion factors and how are they controlled?

## J. Period close and reporting

1. What monthly/quarterly/year-end stock reports are mandatory?
2. Who signs/certifies closing balances?
3. Are prior periods locked in any current system?
4. How are later-discovered errors corrected?
5. What EFY dates/quarters must reports follow?

## K. Evidence capture

For each process photograph/scan:
- blank official form;
- one completed anonymized sample if permissible;
- written procedure/manual page;
- approval/signature matrix;
- report/register sample.

Record source, version/date, issuing authority and whether staff confirm it is currently in force.
