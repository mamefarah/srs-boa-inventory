# Implementation Roadmap

## M0 — Procedure Validation
Collect authoritative procedures/forms, interview stakeholders, complete OFFICIAL_PROCESS_MAPPING, approve unresolved policy decisions.

## M1 — Foundation
Next.js/PWA shell, Supabase local/dev setup, authentication, role/permission foundation, CI, baseline security tests.

## M2 — Item & Warehouse Master
Warehouses/locations, item categories, item master, UOM, funding/project masters, duplicate prevention.

## M3 — Opening Balance
Migration-batch workflow, validated import, reconciliation and locking.

## M4 — Receipt & Inspection
Delivery capture, pending inspection, acceptance/rejection, posting and evidence.

## M5 — Requisition & Approval
Request, review, scoped approval and state transitions.

## M6 — Reservation & Issue
Reservation engine, availability, FEFO, issue posting and acknowledgement.

## M7 — Warehouse Transfer
Dispatch, in-transit, destination receipt and discrepancy handling.

## M8 — Returns & Conditions
Return-to-source linkage, quarantine/damage/expiry state changes.

## M9 — Physical Count
Blind count, cutoff, recount, variance workflow.

## M10 — Adjustments & Reversals
Controlled quantity corrections and immutable reversal flow.

## M11 — Period Close
Reconciliation checklist, close/lock/reopen.

## M12 — Batch/Expiry/Serial
Specialized controls and near-expiry alerting.

## M13 — Disposal
Authorization, evidence and final movement.

## M14 — Reports & Dashboard
Operational/management reports, exports and responsive dashboards.

## M15 — Security Hardening
Threat model, RLS review, negative tests, dependency review, backup/restore.

## M16 — Pilot Readiness
Two-warehouse pilot, training, opening count, UAT, reconciliation, rollback/support plan.
