# M0 Evidence Register — Official Procedure Validation

Status: ACTIVE  
Purpose: distinguish verified regional controlling evidence, Bureau operational evidence, and historical/federal-reference/non-authoritative material before BoA-IMS workflows are frozen.

## Evidence classes

- **A — Regional controlling:** current Somali Regional State / BoFED / BoA approved rule, directive, manual or official form.
- **BOE — Bureau operational evidence:** a document, manual, form, procedure, audit control, or handbook demonstrably used by the Somali Regional State Bureau of Agriculture for its current operations — including operations administered through a Bureau-embedded unit (e.g. an RPCU operating under BoA/BoPAD) or financed in part by a donor/program. Authority: below verified current Somali Region/BoFED controlling law, directive or manual (Class A); above federal-reference-only (Class E), historical/secondary (Class C), and NGO/private/generic reference material (Class F); and, for the specific procedural content it documents, treated as at least as usable as Class B (which shows institutional mandate but no procedural detail). Usable as the current BoA-IMS operational baseline unless and until a verified higher-authority current Somali Region/BoFED source is found that actually contradicts it — the source title containing a project/program name does not by itself demote a document out of this class; what determines the class is whether the document is demonstrably part of the Bureau's own current operational practice, not merely referenced or aspirational.
- **B — Regional institutional evidence:** current official regional source showing mandate/responsibility, but not detailed procedure.
- **C — Historical/secondary regional evidence:** useful to identify older regional rules/documents that must be re-obtained and checked for currency.
- **D — External project-specific material:** a manual or document governing project-financed goods that is *not* demonstrably part of the Bureau's own current operational practice (e.g. administered by a body other than the Bureau, or aspirational/template material not shown to be in live use). Reclassify to BOE once operational use by the Bureau is established.
- **E — Federal reference:** federal rule/manual useful for comparison only unless Somali Region adoption/localization is confirmed.
- **F — Reference-only/non-BoA:** NGO/private/template material; never use as official BoA procedure.

**Evidence-ID migration note (2026-09-24):** former evidence IDs DRDIP-001..007 were renamed BOE-001..007 after confirmation that these sources are Bureau operational evidence, demonstrably used by the Somali Regional State Bureau of Agriculture in its own current operations (administered through an RPCU that operates under, and reports through, the Bureau of Agriculture / Pastoral Development (BoA/BoPAD) structure, on Bureau of Agriculture letterhead — see BOE-005). Original source filenames remain unchanged. Class D is retained in this register for any future source that is genuinely external to the Bureau's own operations (e.g. a different implementing body, or material never shown to be in live Bureau use).

## Evidence rule

A federal manual, NGO template or historical report **cannot silently become a Bureau-wide business rule** merely because it exists — that requires either verified current Somali Region/BoFED controlling status (Class A) or, for material demonstrably used by the Bureau in its own current operations, classification as Bureau operational evidence (Class BOE) per the definition above. Any such classification, and any resulting operational baseline, must be explicitly confirmed in the process mapping and, where material, an ADR — and remains subject to supersession the moment a verified higher-authority current Somali Region/BoFED source is found that actually contradicts it.
